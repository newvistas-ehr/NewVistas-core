# MyHealth Patient Portal — User Guide

## Getting Started

The MyHealth Patient Portal lets you securely access your health records, submit updated health information to your care team, and exchange messages with your providers — all from your computer or mobile device.

**Portal URL:** Your facility will provide the web address for the patient portal (e.g., `https://portal.yourfacility.example`).

---

## Creating Your Account

Before you can use the portal, you need to register. You will need:

- **Your Patient ID** — provided by your care team during registration at the facility. If you do not have your Patient ID, contact the registration desk or your primary care team.
- **An email address** — used for account identification.
- **A password** — must be at least 8 characters long.

### Steps to Register

1. Open the portal URL in your web browser.
2. On the sign-in page, click the **"Register here"** link below the sign-in button.
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Patient ID** — enter the Patient ID given to you by your care team.
   - **Email Address** — enter your personal email.
   - **Display Name** — (optional) how you would like to be addressed in the portal. If left blank, the name on file with your care team will be used.
   - **Password** — choose a password at least 8 characters long.
   - **Confirm Password** — re-enter your password to confirm.
4. Click **"Create Account"**.
5. If successful, you will be signed in automatically and taken to your dashboard.

### Registration Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Patient ID not found" | Verify you are entering the correct ID. Contact your care team if unsure. |
| "An account already exists" | You have already registered. Go back to the sign-in page and log in. |
| "Password must be at least 8 characters" | Choose a longer password. |

---

## Signing In

1. Open the portal URL in your web browser.
2. Enter your **Patient ID** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In"**.
4. You will be taken to your dashboard.

### Forgot Your Password?

Contact your facility's registration desk or your care team to reset your portal password.

---

## The Dashboard

After signing in, you land on your dashboard. It shows:

- **My Profile** — your name, date of birth, email, and last login time.
- **Quick Links** — shortcuts to common actions (submit health info, send a message, view medications, view vitals).
- **Recent Submissions** — your most recent health information submissions and their review status.
- **Unread Messages** — new messages from your care team that you have not read yet.

---

## Viewing Your Health Records

The left sidebar contains links to view your health data. All health record pages are **read-only** — to update your information, use the Health Submissions feature described below.

### My Demographics

View your personal information on file: name, date of birth, address, phone number, emergency contact, and more.

### Medications

View your current and recent medication orders.

### Allergies

View your allergy list, including the allergen, reaction type, and severity.

### Health Conditions

View your active and past health conditions (problem list), including ICD diagnostic codes.

### Vitals

View your most recent vital sign readings (blood pressure, temperature, heart rate, etc.).

### Immunizations

View your immunization history, including vaccine name, date administered, and series information.

## Submitting Health Information

You can submit updated health information for your care team to review. This is useful for reporting address changes, new medications, new allergies, family history updates, and more.

### Creating a New Submission

1. Click **"Health Submissions"** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **"+ New Submission"** button.
3. Fill in any sections that apply. You do not need to fill in every section — empty sections are skipped. Available sections are:
   - **Demographics** — updated address, phone, email, emergency contact, preferred language.
   - **Health Concerns** — describe any new or ongoing health concerns with severity.
   - **Medications** — list medications you are currently taking (including over-the-counter and supplements).
   - **Allergies** — report any new allergies, including the allergen, reaction, and severity.
   - **Health Goals** — enter your personal health goals, one per line (e.g., "Walk 30 minutes daily").
   - **Additional Notes** — free-text notes for your care team.
4. Click each section header to expand or collapse it.
5. To add multiple items (concerns, medications, allergies), click the **"+ Add"** button within each section. To remove an item, click **"Remove"**.
6. When finished, click **"Submit"**.
7. A confirmation message will appear. Your submission is now in the review queue for your care team.

### Submission Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Your submission has been received and is waiting for review. |
| **Under Review** | A clinician is currently reviewing your submission. |
| **Accepted** | All sections of your submission have been accepted and applied to your record. |
| **Partial** | Some sections were accepted and some were not. Check the review notes for details. |
| **Rejected** | Your submission was not accepted. Check the review notes for the reason and resubmit if needed. |

### Viewing a Submission

1. On the Health Submissions page, find the submission in the table.
2. Click **"View"** to see the details.
3. If the submission has been reviewed, you will see the clinician's review notes, including which sections were accepted or rejected.

## Secure Messages

Secure messaging lets you communicate directly with your care team. Messages are private and encrypted.

### Sending a New Message

1. Click **"Secure Messages"** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **"+ New Message"** button.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Subject** — a brief description of your message topic.
   - **Category** — select the category that best fits your question:
     - General
     - Medication Question
     - Appointment
     - Lab Results
     - Referral
     - Billing
   - **Message** — type your message to your care team.
4. Click **"Send Message"**.
5. Your message thread will appear in the conversation list.

### Reading and Replying to Messages

1. On the Secure Messages page, your conversations are listed in a table.
2. Threads with new replies from your care team are marked with a **"New"** badge.
3. Click **"View"** to open the conversation.
4. Messages from you appear on the right (green). Messages from your care team appear on the left (blue).
5. To reply, type your message in the reply box at the bottom and click **"Send Reply"**.
6. To mark provider messages as read, click **"Mark Read"**.

### Message Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | The conversation is active. You and your care team can exchange messages. |
| **Closed** | The conversation has been closed by your care team. If you need to continue the discussion, contact your care team to reopen it or start a new thread. |

## Scheduling Appointments

If your facility has enabled patient self-scheduling, you can view, schedule, cancel, and reschedule appointments directly from the portal.

> **Note:** Not all facilities enable patient self-scheduling. If you do not see the scheduling options described below, contact your care team to manage appointments.

### Checking Feature Availability

Your facility controls which scheduling features are available. The portal will display a message if self-scheduling is not enabled at your site.

### Viewing Your Appointments

1. Click **"Scheduling"** in the left sidebar (if available).
2. Your upcoming appointments are listed with:
   - **Date and Time** of the appointment
   - **Clinic Name** and location
   - **Provider Name** (if assigned)
   - **Status** — Scheduled, Checked In, Completed, Cancelled, or No-Show

### Scheduling a New Appointment

1. Click **"Schedule Appointment"**.
2. Browse available clinics — only clinics that accept patient self-scheduling are shown.
3. Select a clinic and a date to view available time slots.
4. Pick an available slot and fill in:
   - **Purpose** — briefly describe the reason for your visit.
   - **Appointment Type** — select REGULAR or FOLLOW-UP. (URGENT and CONSULT types require staff scheduling.)
5. Click **"Schedule"**.
6. A confirmation message will appear with your appointment details.

### Cancelling an Appointment

1. Find the appointment in your scheduling list.
2. Click **"Cancel"**.
3. Optionally enter a reason for cancellation.
4. Click **"Confirm Cancel"**.
5. If your cancellation is within the notice window (typically 24 hours), you will see a message that your care team has been notified. The cancellation still proceeds.

### Rescheduling an Appointment

1. Find the appointment in your scheduling list.
2. Click **"Reschedule"**.
3. Select a new date, time, and optionally enter a reason.
4. Click **"Confirm Reschedule"**.

### Joining a Waitlist

If no suitable appointment slots are available:

1. Click **"Join Waitlist"** for the clinic you need.
2. Enter:
   - **Desired Appointment Type** — the type of visit you need.
   - **Date Range** — when you are available (optional).
   - **Comments** — any special requirements.
3. Click **"Submit"**.
4. Your entry appears in the waitlist view with status **Waiting**.
5. When a slot becomes available, you may receive an offer. Check your waitlist for offers with status **Offered** and accept or decline.

### Appointment Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | Your appointment is confirmed. |
| **Checked In** | You have checked in at the facility. |
| **Completed** | Your visit is complete. |
| **Cancelled** | The appointment was cancelled (by you, your provider, or the facility). |
| **No-Show** | You did not attend the appointment. |

## Signing Out

1. Click **"Sign Out"** in the top-right corner of the page.
2. You will be returned to the sign-in page.
3. For security, always sign out when using a shared or public computer.

---

## Tips and Best Practices

- **Keep your password secure.** Do not share your Patient ID or password with anyone.
- **Sign out on shared computers.** Always sign out when you are done, especially on public or shared devices.
- **Check your messages regularly.** Your care team may send important information about your care through secure messages.
- **Be specific in submissions.** The more detail you provide in health information submissions, the easier it is for your care team to review and apply changes.
- **One topic per message thread.** Start a new message thread for each topic so your care team can route it to the right person.
- **Use categories.** Selecting the right message category helps your care team prioritize and respond faster.

---

## Getting Help

If you have trouble using the portal:

- **Technical issues** (cannot sign in, page errors) — contact your facility's IT help desk.
- **Account issues** (forgot password, locked out) — contact the registration desk at your facility.
- **Health questions** — use the Secure Messages feature to contact your care team directly.
