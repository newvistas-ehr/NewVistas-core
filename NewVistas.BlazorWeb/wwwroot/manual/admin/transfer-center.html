Transfer Center — moving a patient between hospitals

The Transfer Center (/transfer-center) coordinates placing a patient into a different institution in the health system — for example, Lahey Burlington sending a patient to Lawrence General. It's a request the sending facility submits and the receiving facility controls: the receiver accepts by reserving one of its own beds, or declines. All actions require the DG BED CONTROL key. (Moves within one facility go through ADT; care outside the organization goes through External Referrals.)

Only appears with more than one institution There's nowhere to transfer to at a single-site deployment, so the Transfer Center — and its nav link — hide themselves until the institution directory holds more than one active institution.

Pick your institution

  1. Open /transfer-center. The Institution picker sets whose transfer center you're looking at.
  2. Set it to the receiving hospital to work incoming requests; set it to the sending hospital to see outgoing ones.

Accept an incoming request

  1. On the Incoming tab, click a REQUESTED row to expand its clinical summary, reason, requested bed type, and status timeline.
  2. Click Accept…. An inline bed-picker opens for your institution.
  3. Choose a Unit (each shows how many beds are available), then a Bed. Beds matching the requested type are starred and listed first.
  4. Click Confirm accept. The status becomes ACCEPTED and that bed is now Reserved on your Bed Board — held for this patient only.
Reserve-first The bed is reserved before the request flips to Accepted, so two coordinators can't both grab the last bed. If the reserved bed later becomes unavailable, use Reassign bed… to move the reservation, then complete.

Complete the arrival

  1. When the patient arrives, on the ACCEPTED row click Complete arrival….
  2. Confirm the arrival time; optionally record the receiving attending and admission diagnosis.
  3. Click Confirm arrival.

In one step this discharges the patient at the sending hospital (disposition TRANSFER, its old bed goes to Dirty), admits them into the reserved bed at your hospital, and records your hospital on the patient's treating-facility list — all on the same chart, because a patient carries one identity across every facility in the deployment.

Decline, cancel & new requests

  • Decline (receiving side) — on a REQUESTED row, Decline… with a required reason. No bed was reserved, so nothing is released; a declined request can't later be accepted.
  • Cancel (sending side) — on the Outgoing tab, Cancel… a request you no longer need. Cancelling an accepted transfer releases the receiver's reserved bed.
  • New Request — the New Request tab creates one: load the patient, pick a receiving institution from the placement-target list (only sites that accept transfers and have a matching placeable bed), set level of care / bed type / isolation / urgency, and submit.

The lifecycle at a glance

Status Who sets it What happens next
REQUESTEDSender submitsReceiver accepts (reserves a bed), declines, or sender cancels
ACCEPTEDReceiver, with a reserved bedComplete on arrival, or cancel (releases the bed)
COMPLETEDReceiver, on arrivalTerminal — discharge at sender + admission at receiver recorded
DECLINED / CANCELLEDReceiver / senderTerminal — any reservation is released
