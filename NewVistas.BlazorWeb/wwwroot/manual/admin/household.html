Household — a Person-anchored family unit

The Household (/household) is a family/residential unit of people, anchored on the Person identity (ADR-002) rather than the patient chart. That is the structural difference that matters at registration: a household member who is not a patient still has a place, and one human who appears as a patient, a staff member, and a relative on another chart resolves to the same Person. Gated by the SOCIAL_CARE feature (and, for resolution, PERSON_IDENTITY).

Registration & administration notes

  • Creating a household from a patient bootstraps that patient's Person if needed and makes them head of household.
  • Non-patient members get a bare Person record (name + relationship) — enough to model the family without a full patient registration.
  • Membership is time-bounded: a member who moves out is retained with a "left" date, so household history is preserved (people move between households).
This general social household is distinct from the VA means-test income household (File #408.13), which is a financial, patient-anchored record used for eligibility. The two are complementary.
