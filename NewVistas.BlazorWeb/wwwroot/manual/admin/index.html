Administrator Manual

Everything a system administrator does in NewVistas — moving patients through admission, discharge, and transfer; merging duplicate records; managing the security keys that gate every role; and tuning site-wide configuration — with step-by-step walkthroughs and screenshots. The pages here mirror the Administrative, Financial, and system-administration sections you see in your sidebar after signing in.
