ADT — Admit / Discharge / Transfer

ADT tracks a patient's movements through the facility — admission to a ward, transfer between wards, and discharge — and gives you a live per-ward census and a directory of all wards. It's the equivalent of VistA's ADT (PIMS) movement tracking.

Open ADT and load a patient

  1. In the sidebar, under Administrative, click ADT (or go to /adt).
  2. In the Patient ID field, type the patient ID (e.g. P9001).
  3. Click Load (or press Enter) to list that patient's movements.

The three tabs

Across the top, three tabs switch between the views:

  • Patient Movements — every admit, transfer, and discharge for the loaded patient, with their status.
  • Ward Census — who is currently on a ward you pick.
  • Ward Directory — the searchable list of all wards, their type, division, and bed capacity.

Admit a patient

  1. Load the patient, then click + Admit Patient to open the admission form.
  2. Set the Admission Date/Time (required).
  3. Pick the Ward from the dropdown and enter the Room-Bed (e.g. 301-A).
  4. Optionally add treating specialty, attending physician, admission diagnosis, and comments.
  5. Click Admit. The new movement appears in the table with an ADMITTED badge.
Color = status Movement status badges follow the system-wide scheme: amber for ADMITTED (in-house), blue for TRANSFERRED, and green for DISCHARGED.

Transfer or discharge

Each row that is currently ADMITTED shows two actions in the last column:

  1. Click Transfer to open the transfer form, choose the destination ward and room-bed, optionally update specialty/attending, then click Transfer.
  2. Click Discharge to record a regular discharge as of now.

Ward Census

  1. Click the Ward Census tab.
  2. Pick a ward from the Select Ward dropdown.
  3. The table lists every patient currently on that ward — ID, name, room-bed, specialty, attending, admit date, and last movement.
Tip Need to seed a movement history quickly for a demo? Load a patient and click Demo Load — it records a sample admission and a transfer so the tabs have data to show.
