Emerging Conditions — clusters before they have a code

Emerging-condition surveillance (/emerging-conditions) is for the disease that has no ICD/SNOMED code yet — early COVID, early AIDS. A ProtoCondition is a living, versioned cluster of patients, signals, and hypotheses: you treat and track it locally, close the net as evidence accrues, and promote it to a real code when one arrives. The page is gated by the EMERGING_CONDITIONS feature; creating, editing, promoting, sweeping, confirming and excluding require the EPI MANAGER security key. Reads are open — surveillance is part of the chart, not a privacy silo.

The lifecycle

  1. Define a cluster: a name, then a case definition — features across symptoms, labs, vitals, diagnoses, demographics and exposures, each with a weight and a rule (weighted / must-have / disqualifier). Set a match threshold. Activate to go live.
  2. Screen: run a sweep, or let clinicians surface patients from the symptom survey. Matches arrive as candidates with a full per-feature evidence snapshot (✓ satisfied, ✗ assessed-but-absent, — not assessed).
  3. Review: confirm the candidates that belong and exclude those that don't. Confirming a member can trip a count-threshold alert to the infection-control team.
  4. Close the net: the Analytics tab shows each feature's cluster rate vs its background — a Signal discriminates the cluster, Noise occurs at background. Apply a suggested drop/raise, then re-sweep.
  5. Promote: when a code arrives, promote the cluster. The definition freezes, candidates expire, an eCR trigger is emitted, and every confirmed member is queued for problem-list recoding (with a citation back to the cluster).

Reading the analytics honestly

Rates divide by the patients you actually asked (the assessed denominator), never by the whole cohort — low survey compliance shows up as "not assessed", not as a false Absent. Lift is a documented heuristic with minimum-count guards, not a p-value, and the background source is labeled (assessed population, or the curated catalog when the population is too thin).

Demo: log in as QM3 (CAMPBELL,DIANE S — Infection Preventionist) and open the seeded Novel respiratory cluster (2019). It ships with 9 confirmed + 4 candidate members and an alert armed at 10 — confirming the tenth fires it live. Anosmia reads as a Signal, hearing change as Noise.
