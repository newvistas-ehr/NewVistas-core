Household — the family unit, not just the patient

The Household page (/household) shows a patient's family/residential unit as a set of people. Because it is anchored on the Person identity (not the patient chart), a family member who is not a patient — a child, a spouse not yet registered — still belongs, and a member who is also a staff member or a relative on another chart resolves to the same human. The household outlives any one member: people join and leave, and that history is kept.

Using it

  • If the patient isn't in a household yet, create one (a label + the patient's relationship to the household). The patient becomes the head.
  • Add a non-patient family member by name + relationship — a bare Person record is created for them so they can be referenced without a full registration.
  • Remove a member when they move out — the membership is retained with a "left" date rather than deleted.
The household depends on the Person-identity feature (PERSON_IDENTITY); if a patient has no Person link, the page shows an empty household rather than guessing. This is distinct from the financial means-test income household, which is a separate, money-focused record.
