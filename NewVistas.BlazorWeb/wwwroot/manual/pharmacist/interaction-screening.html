Drug Interaction Blocking

The Drug Interaction Blocking screen runs a new prescription against the patient's existing medications, records every interaction it finds, and blocks the fill on any significant or contraindicated pairing until a pharmacist overrides it with a clinical reason.

Load a patient's screenings

  1. From the sidebar, open Interaction Screening (or go to /interaction-blocking).
  2. In the Patient ID field, type the patient ID and press Enter or click Load.
  3. The screenings load into tabbed views: All and Blocked, each with a count.
  4. If you arrived with a patient already in context, the screen auto-loads them.

What you see

The screening list

Each row in the All and Blocked tabs shows:

  • Date — when the screen was run.
  • Drug and Rx ID — the prescription that was screened.
  • Status — Not Screened, Cleared, Blocked, or Overridden.
  • Blocking and Total — the count of blocking interactions and total interactions found.
  • View — opens the Detail tab for that screening.

A blocked screening's row is highlighted, and its blocking count is shown in red.

The detail panel

The Detail tab lists every Interaction Finding — the new drug ingredient, the existing drug ingredient, a severity badge (Minor, Moderate, Significant, Contraindicated), a description, and whether the finding is BLOCKED, a Warning, or already overridden. A cleared prescription shows "No drug interactions found — prescription is cleared."

Screen a new prescription

  1. With a patient loaded, click + Screen Rx.
  2. Fill in the required Prescription ID and Drug Name.
  3. Optionally add the new and existing ingredient IEN/name pairs to test, plus the Screened By pharmacist ID.
  4. Click Run Screen. The screening is created, the success banner shows its ID, and the Detail tab opens automatically.

Override a blocking interaction

  1. On a blocked finding, click Override.
  2. Enter the required Pharmacist ID and a Clinical Reason.
  3. Click Submit Override. The finding flips to Overridden by your pharmacist ID, and the screening status moves to Overridden.
⚠ Both fields are required An override will not submit without a pharmacist ID and a clinical reason — the reason is stored verbatim against the finding so the override is accountable. Only the prescriber-of-record's pharmacist should clear a contraindicated pairing.
Blocked means blocked While a finding is blocking and not yet overridden, the prescription is held in Blocked status. Clearing or overriding every blocking finding is what releases the fill on the Verify & Fill screen.
