Pharmacist Manual

Everything a pharmacist does in NewVistas — verifying and filling prescriptions, running Drug Utilization Review, processing controlled substances through EPCS, and adjudicating point-of-sale insurance claims — with step-by-step walkthroughs and screenshots. The pages here mirror the Pharmacy section you see in your sidebar after signing in.

In this manual

Getting StartedSign in, navigate, and pull up a patient. Verify & FillVerify, fill, refill, hold, and discontinue prescriptions. Drug Utilization ReviewScreen for duplicates, allergies, dose, and organ-function risks. EPCSReceive, sign, and manage controlled-substance e-prescriptions. Pharmacy HubThe pharmacy operations center and its modules. Interaction ScreeningSeverity-rated drug interactions and overrides. Inpatient MedsUnit-dose / IV orders, verification, and admin. Drug AccountabilityStock, the signed ledger, and receive/dispense/waste. Benefits & PAPlan coverage checks and prior-authorization review. POS ClaimsSubmit and adjudicate point-of-sale insurance claims.
Before you start You'll need a pharmacist account (e.g. PHARM1) and the system running (SiloHost, WebServer, and the Blazor app). See Getting Started.
