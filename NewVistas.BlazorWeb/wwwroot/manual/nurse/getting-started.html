Getting Started

Sign in, find your way around the role-scoped menu, and pull up a patient.

1. Sign in first

NewVistas is login-first: you cannot see any clinical screens — or even the menu of features — until you authenticate. Opening any address takes you straight to the Sign-In screen.

  1. Enter your Access Code (username), e.g. NURSE4.
  2. Enter your Verify Code (password).
  3. Click Sign In (or press Enter).
VistA terms "Access Code" and "Verify Code" are VistA's names for username and password — the labels long-time CPRS users expect.

2. Your menu is scoped to your role

After sign-in you land on the home screen. The left sidebar shows only the areas your security keys grant. A nurse sees Clinical, Nursing, Laboratory, Radiology, and Administrative; a pharmacist would see Pharmacy instead. You won't be shown features you can't use.

3. Pull up a patient

Most screens work on a selected patient. Use Patient Lookup (top of the sidebar) to search, or type a patient ID directly into the lookup bar on any clinical page.

  1. Click Patient Lookup in the sidebar.
  2. Type the patient ID (e.g. 9) or name.
  3. Open the patient, or go straight to a nursing screen and enter the ID in its lookup bar, then click Load.
Tip Once a patient is loaded, a context bar at the top of the content area keeps their name and ID visible as you move between screens.
