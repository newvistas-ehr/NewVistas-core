Symptom Survey — the wide net at the bedside

Nursing is the front door for the coded symptom survey (/symptom-survey). During the admission or shift assessment, you record a structured review-of-systems where every answer is Present, Absent, or Unknown — the input that lets the epidemiology team detect and tighten an emerging-disease cluster. Your ORELSE key is enough to record answers.

The one rule that matters

Unknown means "we didn't ask." It is never recorded as Absent. Mark Present or Absent only for what you actually assessed; the honesty of the "Absent" answers is exactly what lets a common symptom later be dropped from a cluster's definition. Repeated inpatient assessments also capture onset and progression — signal an outpatient snapshot can't.

Steps

  1. Open Surveillance → Symptom Survey, enter the patient, and Load.
  2. Work through the body-system groups; answer what you assessed, leave the rest Unknown.
  3. Save answers.
  4. Optionally Screen against active clusters; if the patient matches, use Suggest into cluster to flag them for the epidemiologist.
If the patient is later confirmed into an active cluster, an isolation precaution banner appears on their cover sheet — check it before you room or transport them.
